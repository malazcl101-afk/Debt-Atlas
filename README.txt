HARBORNESTDIGITAL · DEBT ATLAS
QUICK START & BUYER GUIDE

1. Unzip this package. Open index.html in a current browser on your computer. For a phone, use the hosted demo or place these files together on an HTTPS static host. Local file URLs on mobile browsers may limit storage and installation.
2. Add each debt from Debts & accounts. Enter the opening recorded balance, balance record date, APR, minimum and due day.
3. Enter a monthly debt payment budget in Payoff strategies. Choose snowball, avalanche or custom order. If the amount does not cover minimums, the app shows why it cannot produce a payoff date.
4. Record payments, including regular and extra portions. Principal defaults to total minus recorded interest. You can correct principal to reflect the lender statement.
5. Reconcile balances from an account workspace using a statement snapshot. Dated rate changes affect future projections.
6. Review the calendar, charts, goals, tasks and monthly reflections. The what-if calculator does not edit saved payment records.
7. Choose Ivory, Sage, Peach, Lilac or Midnight in Customize & backup. Select display settings, extra contrast, currency or save a custom accent theme.
8. Download a JSON backup regularly. Restore it from Customize & backup to transfer records to another browser/device. CSV exports are for spreadsheet review.

PRIVACY / OFFLINE
No login, bank link, cloud sync or subscription. Data stays in IndexedDB in the browser you use. It is not automatically shared across devices. Files work offline on a desktop after download; HTTPS hosting enables install prompts and service-worker caching where supported. Clearing browser data or using a private session may erase records. Keep backups separately. A downloaded backup contains financial data.

MATH & LIMITS
Projected schedules assume entered balances and rates, monthly interest calculations, fixed budget, priority order and no new borrowing or fees. They are estimates only. An insufficient budget or non-decreasing debt returns an explanation instead of a date. Lenders may calculate interest daily or allocate payments differently. The app sends no payments or alerts and gives no financial advice.

FILES
index.html, style.css, app.js, icon PNGs, manifest.webmanifest and sw.js. Keep them together. The demo ZIP contains fictional data in a separate browser database. This demo starts with fictional records. Demo data never appears in the customer edition.
